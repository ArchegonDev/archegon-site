Four problems.
(a) The wordmark is live <text> set in Snell Roundhand, Apple Chancery, Brush Script MT, Segoe Script, cursive — so it renders as a different typeface on Mac, Windows and Android. Your logo is not a fixed shape. It must be converted to outlines.
(b) The blue is #1b54a8. The site is ember #d66b35 on ink. Nothing on the page shares that hue.
(c) The grey fill #e6e6e6 is invisible on paper and an obvious patch on dark grounds.
(d) A copperplate script reads luxury-brand, not deep drilling. Fervo's credibility comes from looking engineered.
