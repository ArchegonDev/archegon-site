What changed after our stress test (12 Oct 2026)

We stress-tested the deck, model and diagrams as a sceptical investor and engineer would. As presented, the plan did not pass. Every point raised is tracked and the materials here are the corrected v3:

  • One definition of net and gross. Every MWe-per-producer figure is net at the plant fence. The 6.25 MW at the fence is average power, not firm.
  • Power and data centre priced separately. The generation system's levelised cost is A$221–300/MWh at 6–10% (A$259 at 8%), about what the hall earns. There is no power-only fallback on an islanded site.
  • The return is stated openly. At the 5 MW reference, contribution is 3.3% of the capital: below any cost of capital.
  • Break-even is called break-even. A$101.3m is an operating break-even hall, not a viable PoC. The smallest build in whole 1.25 MW modules is A$105.7m.
  • Reservoir first. The first capital tranche is a ~A$52m doublet and circulation test, with pass/fail criteria, before any hall is built.
  • Flow basis shown. 5 MWe net per producer needs roughly 64–97 kg/s at 215 °C; the Cooper Basin's best sustained loop (Habanero) was 19 kg/s. A site flow test decides this.

Illustrative only. Figures are drawn from public benchmarks and from Archegon's concept model, labelled by status (VERIFIED, ESTIMATE, DERIVED, ASSUMPTION, RATE_MISSING). Unpriced items ship at A$0, so every capital figure is a floor. Nothing on this site is an offer of securities, a solicitation or financial advice.
