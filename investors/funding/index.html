How this is calculated, and what it leaves out

Runway (24 months × A$250k) is funded first, then 25% contingency on every capex line. For each whole number of producers (one injector each), the remaining budget buys ORC plant and data-centre modules in whole 1.25 MW steps, capped at what the producers can supply (producers × net MWe × 0.90 ÷ PUE 1.25). The tier picks the producer count with the best contribution per dollar committed and leaves the rest undrawn rather than buying a second doublet for a sliver of capacity. Electricity shown at the fence is the average (× 0.90 capacity factor); firm power with one producer out is shown separately. Contribution = revenue − plant O&M − team; it is not a return.

Unpriced (A$0, so every figure is a floor): land, brine gathering, fibre, native title, heritage and tenement legals, well replacement, data-centre opex, battery, standby generation, black start, stimulation and test facilities. Revenue assumes a full hall from day one at a US primary-market colocation rate; an islanded remote site may price lower. The 5 MWe net per producer is an assumption; the Cooper Basin's best sustained loop (Habanero, 19 kg/s) supports roughly 1–1.5 MWe net.
