T3 buys Gate 1 long-lead items: a baseline microseismic network (8 stations; USArray station cost, DOE protocol), a stimulation water bore (about 19 ML per stimulation, Utah FORGE 2024) and the balance towards casing, wellheads and a rig-slot deposit. The balance is a derived figure, checked to stay below the cost of the two Gate 1 wells. Every dollar of T3 reduces the Gate 1 round. If a gate fails, unreleased tranches are not drawn.

G4: what a tenant LOI has to price

At a 10% hurdle (assumption) over 25 years, a priced letter of intent passes only at or above US$509/kW/month for colocation (model benchmark US$172.5) or US$6.66/GPU-hour for compute at flat prices (model base US$5.52). Below that, the project stops before Gate 1.

Gate 1 round

A$69.23m: one doublet with stimulation, rig mobilisation and 25% contingency, plus runway, less the T3 items already bought, grossed up for a 6% success fee (A$4.15m), paid from that round.

Go/no-go gates before the next round

  • G1 Resource: Third-party P50 case (investor-appointed engineer) meets the Flow-Basis flow for 5 MWe net per producer, with a decline forecast method agreed; Gate 1 test plan carries the Gated-Plan GO/NO-GO limits
  • G2 Cost: AFE for the Gate 1 doublet incl. stimulation within the Gated-Plan C6 budget; if over, stop or re-scope (the budget is not re-set to pass)
  • G3 Tenure, licence, government: Licence pathway confirmed; ILUA heads of terms signed; GAB water licence pathway; SA Government / ARENA support letter
  • G4 Commercial: Priced LOI for at least one module at >= US$509/kW/month colo or >= US$6.66/GPU-h, the prices that give a 10% 25-yr IRR
  • G5 Connectivity and power design: Fibre access agreed or priced; N-1 power design (third doublet, two ORC trains) quoted; ORC derate curve
  • G6 Financing: Gate 1 lead investor, ARENA or strategic soft-circled for the Gated-Plan C6 round
